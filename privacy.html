Privacy Policy

How Luxy AI by Afraz Studio collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Luxy AI (“the App”), developed and published by Afraz Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Luxy AI mobile application and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By downloading, installing, or using Luxy AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.
  • Usage Data: Features used, session duration, crash logs, and performance metrics to improve the App.
  • Optional Inputs: Content you voluntarily provide within the App (e.g., prompts, preferences, or settings).
  • Technical Logs: IP address (anonymized where possible), browser type, and network information for security and diagnostics.

Luxy AI is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account to use core features, and we minimize data collection to what is necessary for the App to function and improve.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service

  • Diagnose technical issues and prevent abuse
  • Respond to support requests when you contact us

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data is transmitted over secure channels where applicable.

Where processing occurs on-device, data remains under your control. Any server-side processing is limited to what is required for the feature you are using.

The App may interact with third-party services (for example, AI model providers or analytics tools) solely to deliver functionality. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and contractual obligations that restrict use of data beyond the purpose of serving the App.

6. Children’s Privacy

Luxy AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
